Privacy Policy

How Cornerstone handles information on this website and in the Family Portal.

Draft for review by the school and its legal counsel — not yet in effect.

Last updated: draft, November 2026

Who we are

Brazos Valley Cornerstone Christian Academy (“Cornerstone”, “the school”), 3200 Cavitt Avenue, Bryan, Texas 77801, runs this website and the Family Portal. Questions about privacy: office@bvcca.com or (979) 694-8200.

This website

The public website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser keeps only your language choice, on your own device.

The admissions inquiry form sends nothing to us by itself: it opens your own email app, and you decide whether to send the message.

What the Family Portal holds

  • Account details for parents, guardians and staff: name, email address, role and preferred language.
  • Student records the school already keeps: name, grade, student ID, grades, attendance, document requests and issued documents.
  • Messages between families and school staff, and school announcements.

How we use it

Only to run the school and to communicate with families: sharing grades and attendance, answering messages, issuing requested documents and sending notices. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Parents and guardians see only their own children’s records. Teachers see the students in their own classes. Office staff see what they need to run the school. These limits are enforced by the system itself (row-level security), not only by the screens you see.

Student education records

Where the U.S. Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) applies, parents — and students once they turn 18 — may inspect education records, ask for corrections, and must give consent before records are shared outside the school, except where the law allows. Questions can be raised with the school office.

Children

Portal accounts are for parents, guardians and school staff. Students do not create accounts, and we do not knowingly collect personal information online directly from children under 13.

Service providers

The portal is hosted by Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage) and the website by GitHub Pages. They process information only to provide those services to the school.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Issued documents are kept in private storage and downloaded through short-lived private links. Portal accounts are by invitation only.

How long we keep it

Records are kept as long as the school needs them and as the law requires. When a family leaves, portal access is closed; school records follow the school’s records-retention policy.

Your choices

You can change your name and language in the portal, ask the office to correct information, or ask to close your account by writing to office@bvcca.com.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new date and tell portal users about important changes.
