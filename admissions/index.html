Parent and Student Handbook

How school works.

The 2026–27 handbook covers the school day, attendance, grading, conduct, the dress code and more. Please read it together as a family.

School day

8:00 AM – 3:15 PM. Core classes for K–5 end at 1:45 PM, followed by enrichment.

Drop-off and pick-up

Doors open at 7:30 AM. Cars line up on Willow Street, passenger side toward campus.

Lunch

Brought from home and ready to eat; there is no microwave. Kindergartners also bring a daily snack.

Grading

Grades 1–5: A+ to F, with plus and minus. Grades 6–12: A 90–100, B 80–89, C 70–79, F below 70.

Uniforms and Dress Code

Dressed for the work.

School is a place of work, and how we dress matters. In the community, a uniform represents Cornerstone.

Shirts

Polo shirts in white, gold or black with the school logo, tucked in.

Bottoms

Black or khaki dress pants, shorts, skirts or jumpers. No denim or cargo pockets; knee length.

Shoes

Closed-toe shoes for outdoor play. No flip-flops, sandals or clogs.

Fridays

Spirit day: neat jeans with an approved Cornerstone T-shirt.

Field trips

The Cougar T-shirt, unless the teacher says otherwise.

Outerwear

Coats, sweaters or hoodies worn indoors must be Cornerstone-approved or carry the school logo.
